WHO WE ARE

Where Business Meets Sustainability.

EcoMetric is an environmental consulting and sustainable product development brand that aims to help businesses understand and reduce their environmental impact.

We believe that sustainability should not be complicated or difficult to achieve. Through practical solutions, environmental awareness, and innovative ideas, we want to make it easier for businesses to adopt greener practices while continuing to grow.

Our goal is to connect business innovation with environmental responsibility, creating opportunities for a future where both businesses and the planet can thrive.

OUR STORY

A Small Idea With a Bigger Purpose.

EcoMetric started with a simple idea: businesses should not have to choose between growth and protecting the environment.

As environmental challenges continue to grow, we believe businesses have an important role in creating positive change. However, taking the first step toward sustainability can sometimes feel overwhelming.

That is why EcoMetric envisions making the process simpler. By combining technology, business, and environmental sustainability, we aim to provide solutions that help organizations understand their impact and discover better ways to operate.

BEHIND THE BRAND

Meet the Founder.

The Vision Behind EcoMetric

EcoMetric was envisioned by Kyle Coloma, a Computer Science student with a strong interest in technology, entrepreneurship, and environmental sustainability.

With the belief that technology can be used to solve real-world problems, the idea behind EcoMetric is to explore how digital innovation and business solutions can contribute to a more sustainable future.

The vision is simple: make sustainability more accessible, practical, and achievable for businesses of all sizes.
